# 800 to the Mile

My entry for the **#30DayMapChallenge 2026**: thirty maps of Chicago, one a day through November,
about who gets to move through the city and who is held back.

**View the atlas:** https://amitrm.github.io/2026-30d-maps/

## The challenge

[#30DayMapChallenge](https://30daymapchallenge.com/) is a community event held every November.
Each day has a theme, and mapmakers around the world share one map per theme. Any tools and any data are allowed.

## Why Chicago, and why this story

Chicago's street grid makes a simple promise: 800 address numbers to a mile, a major street every
half mile, and a bus on nearly every major street. On paper, every mile of the city is equal.

I work with transportation data, and I wanted to spend this year's challenge testing that promise
from the point of view of the people it serves least: riders who cannot take the stairs, neighborhoods
at the edge of the network, and trips that take twice as long as the distance suggests.
Each map uses public open data, and the whole set reads as one story told in five parts.

1. **The Grid** – the system as it was built and promised
2. **The Systems** – how the city actually moves, and who decides
3. **The Gaps** – where the promise breaks
4. **The Body** – travel as a physical experience
5. **The Possible** – what the city could be
