chat window or on the command line". Plate X was made that way: in one command-line session with an
AI coding assistant, which proposed the ideas, wrote and ran the notebook (`day10.ipynb`), wrote the
plate renderer and checked the numbers and claims. These are every prompt typed in that session for
Day 10, verbatim and in order. Nothing in the data, the code or the plate was edited by hand.
